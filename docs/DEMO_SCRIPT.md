# PROBAHO (প্রবাহ) — 3-Minute Demo Pitch Script

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Crisis (Problem)
*"Bangladesh manufactures over 95% of its finished medicines, yet public health facilities regularly experience severe stockouts of essential drugs, while expired medicines pile up in neighboring clinics. Why? Because the country imports over 85% of its Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients from abroad, and health managers have no predictive tool connecting upstream port delays with clinic-shelf stock."*

## 0:30 - 1:00 | The Solution (PROBAHO Overview)
*"Introducing PROBAHO (প্রবাহ) — the AI-powered decision support platform for Bangladesh's medicine supply chain. It models the chain from imported Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient shipments through factory and warehouse to healthcare clinics. It forecasts demand, simulates disruption propagation, flags expiring batches, and recommends optimal stock transfers in Bangla and English with mandatory human approval."*

## 1:00 - 2:00 | Live Walkthrough (P0 Demo Path)
1. **Executive Dashboard:** *"Here on the Dashboard, national availability sits below target, with critical stockouts in Pirganj and high upstream shipment volatility from our Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient suppliers."*
2. **Digital Twin & Disruption Simulator:** *"Let's introduce a 14-day shipment delay and 30% supplier shortfall in the Disruption Simulator. Instantly, we see the discrete ripple effect: manufacturer stock depletes by Day 12, warehouse reserves drop by Day 20, and Pirganj stocks out of Paracetamol by Day 28."*
3. **Smart Redistribution:** *"Instead of allowing stockouts, PROBAHO's OR-Tools optimizer computes feasible transfers: Morrelganj has surplus Paracetamol near expiry. The optimizer proposes moving 400 units to Pirganj, strictly preserving Morrelganj's safety stock and verifying transport lead time."*
4. **Bangla AI Copilot:** *"The copilot explains the clinical reasoning in Bangla: 'পীরগঞ্জে প্যারাসিটামলের মজুদ ৩ দিনে শেষ হবে, তাই মোড়েলগঞ্জ থেকে ৪০০ ইউনিট স্থানান্তর সুপারিশ করা হলো।'"*
5. **Approval & Audit:** *"I approve the recommendation with my name and reason. It immediately records a block in our SHA-256 tamper-evident audit log. Clicking 'Verify Chain' proves zero tampering across the network."*

## 2:00 - 2:30 | Technology & Responsible AI
*"All quantities and transfers are computed by deterministic statistical forecasting and Google OR-Tools. The LLM explains only — zero hallucinated arithmetic. All data is synthetic and clearly labeled."*

## 2:30 - 3:00 | Impact & Conclusion
*"PROBAHO turns supply chain shocks into proactive, auditable rebalancing actions before patients ever face an empty clinic shelf. Thank you."*
