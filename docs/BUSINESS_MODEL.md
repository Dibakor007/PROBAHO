# PROBAHO (প্রবাহ) — Business Model & Scaling Strategy

## 1. Value Proposition
PROBAHO bridges the critical gap between upstream import dependency and downstream clinic-shelf stockouts in Bangladesh's healthcare network. By anticipating shortages and proposing constrained redistributions, it reduces essential medicine stockouts and minimizes shelf-expiry waste.

## 2. Target Customers & Partners
- **Public Sector:** Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Central Medical Stores Depot (CMSD), Essential Drugs Company Limited (EDCL).
- **Private & NGO Healthcare Networks:** Large hospital groups (Square, Evercare, United) and community health networks (BRAC, Gonoshasthaya Kendra).
- **Pharmaceutical Manufacturers:** Local formulators tracking raw Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient delivery risks and hospital demand signals.

## 3. Revenue Models (Hypothesized)
1. **SaaS Subscription:** Tiered monthly license per healthcare facility or district warehouse.
2. **Enterprise Supply Chain Intelligence:** Advanced risk analytics and disruption forecasting for pharmaceutical manufacturers and international distributors.
3. **Deployment & Integration Services:** Custom adapters for hospital ERPs and DGHS eLMIS inventory feeds.

## 4. Go-To-Market & Pilot Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Discovery & Shadow Pilot):** 1 Upazila Health Complex (e.g. Pirganj) and 4 feeder Community Clinics tracking 20 tracer medicines in shadow-recommendation mode.
- **Phase 2 (Regional Expansion):** Deployment across 1 complete division (e.g. Rangpur) with active redistribution approvals.
- **Phase 3 (National Scaling):** Integration with national eLMIS and customs import clearance APIs.
