# Limitations — PROBAHO (প্রবাহ)

PROBAHO is a hackathon prototype. This document transparently describes its current limitations and scope boundaries.

---

## 1. Synthetic Data Only

All data in the system is generated synthetically by `seed/generator.py` with a fixed random seed (42). It does **not** represent:

- Real medicine consumption patterns in Bangladesh.
- Actual facility locations, capacities, or stock levels.
- Real supplier, manufacturer, or logistics data.
- Any government or DGDA data.

**Implication:** Forecast accuracy metrics, simulation outputs, and optimisation results are demonstrations of the pipeline — not predictions about real supply chains.

---

## 2. No Real Supply Chain Connection

PROBAHO has no integration with any real-world system:

- No connection to DGDA (Directorate General of Drug Administration).
- No connection to facility inventory management systems.
- No live data feeds from suppliers or logistics providers.
- No connection to any government or national health system.

The system operates entirely on its own synthetic dataset.

---

## 3. LLM Explanations May Be Imperfect

The AI copilot uses Gemini to generate natural-language explanations of structured data. While a validation layer checks that all numbers in the response trace back to tool outputs:

- **Phrasing may be misleading.** The LLM might imply causation where the data only shows correlation.
- **Context may be lost.** The LLM does not have domain expertise in Bangladeshi public health logistics.
- **Hallucination risk.** Despite number validation, qualitative statements (e.g., "this is due to monsoon season") are generated by the LLM and may not be accurate.

**Mitigation:** The fallback template system provides guaranteed-accurate (if less fluent) explanations when Gemini is unavailable or when validation fails.

---

## 4. Free-Tier Deployment Limitations

The recommended deployment targets (Vercel free tier + HuggingFace Spaces free tier) have operational constraints:

| Limitation | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **Cold starts** | HuggingFace Spaces may take 30–60 seconds to wake from sleep |
| **Memory** | Free-tier containers have limited RAM; large datasets may cause OOM |
| **Compute** | OR-Tools solver may time out on large problem instances |
| **Rate limits** | Gemini free-tier API has request-per-minute limits |
| **Availability** | Free-tier services offer no SLA; expect occasional downtime |
| **Concurrency** | Single-worker deployment; concurrent users may experience slowness |

---

## 5. No Patient Data or Clinical Advice

PROBAHO is a **logistics decision-support tool**. It does not:

- Store, process, or display any patient data.
- Provide clinical advice, dosing recommendations, or treatment guidance.
- Replace professional medical or pharmaceutical judgment.
- Handle any personally identifiable information (PII).

---

## 6. Single-User, No Authentication (P0)

The P0 prototype has no authentication or authorisation:

- Any user who can reach the URL can access all features.
- The audit trail records a user-provided name, but does not verify identity.
- There is no role-based access control (RBAC).

**P1 scope:** JWT-based authentication with role-based permissions.

---

## 7. Optimisation Is Demonstrative

The redistribution optimiser demonstrates the approach but is not production-grade:

- **Scale:** Tested with 6 facilities × 5 medicines. Performance on larger networks is untested.
- **Constraints:** Real supply chain constraints (vehicle capacity, driver availability, road conditions, regulatory requirements) are not modelled.
- **Cost model:** Transport costs are simplified to a single per-unit rate per route.
- **Solver limits:** The CP-SAT solver has a time limit to prevent blocking; near-optimal solutions may be returned instead of proven optima.

---

## 8. Forecasting Model Limitations

- **No external regressors:** Models do not incorporate weather data, public health campaigns, holidays, or other factors that affect real demand.
- **Fixed seasonality:** The Holt-Winters model assumes weekly seasonality. Monthly or annual patterns require model tuning not included in P0.
- **Short history:** The synthetic dataset covers 12 months. Real deployment would benefit from longer training windows.
- **No anomaly detection:** Demand spikes from outbreaks or emergencies are not explicitly detected or handled.

---

## 9. Simulation Simplifications

- **Linear network:** The simulator models a single supplier → manufacturer → warehouse → facilities chain. Real networks are more complex.
- **Deterministic demand:** Simulation uses forecast or historical demand, not stochastic draws. Real demand has irreducible randomness.
- **No multi-sourcing:** Each node has a single upstream supplier. Real supply chains often have backup sources.
- **No inventory policies:** The simulator does not model reorder points, economic order quantities, or procurement lead times.

---

## 10. Browser Support

The frontend is tested on modern desktop browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge). It is not optimised for:

- Mobile viewports (responsive design is P1).
- Older browsers (IE, legacy Edge).
- Assistive technologies beyond basic keyboard navigation and colour contrast.
